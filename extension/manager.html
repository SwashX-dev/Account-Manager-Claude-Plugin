Your accounts

Sign in once on Claude’s official website. This extension never asks for passwords or tokens.

Sessions & privacy

Labels are yours to choose, not verified account identities. “Current space” means the container in the active tab, not confirmed login status.

To log out, open an account and use Claude’s own profile → Log out. If a session expires, sign in there again. Firefox stores the sessions; this manager stores only names and container IDs.

Removing a space closes all its tabs and deletes its container data. It does not delete your Claude account or revoke sessions on other devices.
